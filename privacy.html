Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Lenovo Paperwork Importer is a private, single-user utility operated solely by its owner. It is not available to the public.

Google Drive access

The utility requests read-only Google Drive access so it can list and download files selected by the owner. It cannot upload, edit, rename, move or delete Google Drive content.

Data handling

Downloaded files are transferred to the owner's privately operated Lenovo system for local document processing. The utility does not sell data, serve advertising, profile users or disclose Google user data to third parties.

Retention and control

Data is retained only within systems controlled by the owner. The owner may revoke access at any time through Google Account security settings.

Scope

This policy applies only to the Lenovo Paperwork Importer OAuth client.
