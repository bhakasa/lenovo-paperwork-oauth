Lenovo HHJ Exchange

Lenovo HHJ Exchange is a private, single-owner desktop utility being configured for secure transfer of owner-approved files between a personally operated Lenovo Linux computer and the owner's separate HHJ Google Drive workspace. It is not a public service.

The application uses Google Drive OAuth to access the owner's HHJ account with permissions approved by the owner. Its intended operation is limited to designated exchange folders for sending and receiving approved material; actual OAuth permissions are governed by the consent screen and granted scopes. The utility is not currently advertised as operational.

The utility does not provide cloud AI services with direct access to the owner's separate private BUK Google account. Raw bank statements, credentials and unsanitised financial records are not intended for transfer into the HHJ workspace.
