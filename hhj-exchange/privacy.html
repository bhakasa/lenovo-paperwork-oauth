Privacy Policy — Lenovo HHJ Exchange

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Lenovo HHJ Exchange is a private, single-owner application for moving owner-approved files between an owner-controlled Lenovo computer and the owner's HHJ Google Drive account. It is not offered to the public.

Google Drive access

The application requests Google OAuth access to the HHJ Google Drive account for file transfer, receipt verification and recovery. It may read file metadata and file content and create or update exchange records according to the scopes and permissions the owner explicitly grants. The intended target is the designated HHJ exchange folders; Google OAuth scopes themselves may permit access beyond those folders. No access to the owner's separate private BUK Google account is requested by this HHJ OAuth application.

What information is processed

Only files and metadata selected for the owner's exchange workflow, including filenames, document identifiers, hashes, timestamps and transfer/verification receipts, are intended for processing. Source bank statements, original sensitive banking documents, passwords and OAuth tokens must not be placed in exchanged content.

Where information goes

Approved data is handled on the owner's Lenovo computer and HHJ Google Drive. The owner may separately authorise connected services, including AI tools, to view material stored in HHJ. The exchange application does not automatically grant them access to BUK data. Transfers use Google's services as part of Google Drive operation. No data is sold, used for advertising or disclosed to unrelated parties by this utility.

Retention and security

Documents stay within the owner's selected Google Drive folders and controlled local systems until the owner removes them. Transfer receipts and operational logs may be retained to prevent duplicate delivery and diagnose failures. OAuth credentials remain in restricted local configuration and are not published on this website or in exchanged documents.

User control

The owner can remove files, stop the application and revoke its Google account access using Google Account security settings. Access is granted only through the owner's own Google OAuth consent.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information obtained from Google APIs is limited to providing the owner-requested exchange function. The application does not use Google data for advertising, sale, or unrelated profiling.

Scope

This policy applies only to the Lenovo HHJ Exchange OAuth application. It does not replace the separate Lenovo Paperwork Importer privacy policy.
